📚 About VIS Academy

Velocity International STEM Academy (VIS-Academy) is an international online STEM academy dedicated to making Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics easier, practical and enjoyable.

Our Mission

To inspire learners, develop problem-solving skills and build academic excellence through engaging STEM education.

Our Vision

To shape the next generation of scientists, engineers, innovators and technology leaders. Established: 2020
